Vectrex N64 Conversion Tools

Make the data files needed for the pw64vec and mk64vec from your own copies of the games.

Your files stay on your computer. The conversion runs inside this page; your ROM and music are never uploaded anywhere. Use your own copies, and don't share what the tools make.

1 · Pilotwings 64 ROM → ISL, TASKS, TEXT, SFX (and music 16–29)

Drop your Pilotwings 64 (USA) ROM. Accepts .z64, .v64 and .n64.

2 · Soundtrack mp3s → BGM00–29.WAV

Pick all the soundtrack mp3s at once (names like 05. Hang Glider.mp3). Files with unknown names are left out. Takes a minute or two.

Then

  1. Unzip the download. You get a folder called pw64vec (or mk64vec).
  2. Copy its files into the same-named folder on the cartridge's SD card, next to the game's .um2 file.
  3. Made both zips? Merge them into one pw64vec folder.
